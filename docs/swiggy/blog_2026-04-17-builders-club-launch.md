# Swiggy Announces Builders Club - Turning AI Commerce Infrastructure into a Builder Ecosystem

> A new community and partnership program for developers, startups and enterprises building on top of Swiggy's MCP stack.

> **Bengaluru, India.** Swiggy announced that it is launching soon **Swiggy Builders Club**, a new community, partnership, and builder-enablement program designed for developers, startups and enterprises building AI-native commerce experiences on top of Swiggy's platforms.

While Swiggy's earlier MCP launch opened up the core commerce infrastructure, Builders Club is a distinct next step: it is the **ecosystem layer** around that infrastructure - focused on who builds, how they get access, how they collaborate with Swiggy, what skills they can contribute, and how standout integrations scale into real partnerships.

When it launches, Builders Club will open access to **3 MCP servers and 18+ API tools** across **Food**, **Instamart**, and **Dineout** - enabling approved builders to create AI agents, copilots, assistants, and integrations that can take real-world actions like ordering food, shopping groceries, or booking dining experiences.

## What Builders Club is

Builders Club is Swiggy's program for bringing external builders into the Swiggy AI ecosystem - not just as API consumers, but as contributors, partners, and early ecosystem participants. The initiative is positioned around community builders, partnerships, skills, and an invite-led experience designed to bring in serious developers, founders, and enterprise teams who want to build with Swiggy in an AI-first world.

## A call to the community to build

Swiggy is positioning Builders Club as an open invitation to the broader builder ecosystem - developers, startups, and enterprises who want to create practical AI commerce experiences can apply, get access, and start shipping on top of Swiggy's APIs and MCP services.

## The role of Skills

A major part of Builders Club is its connection to **Skills** - reusable capabilities that help AI agents handle real-world tasks more effectively. Swiggy's broader ecosystem narrative brings together **MCP integrations**, **Real World Skills**, and an upcoming **Builders Platform**, making Builders Club the place where these pieces come together for external builders.

## Who can request access, and how

Builders Club is open to developers, startups, and enterprises. The builder journey is:

1. **Build locally** - prototype on `http://localhost` against a stub or staging endpoint. You don't need credentials to start.
2. **Record a video** - a short screen capture of your agent flow working end-to-end.
3. **Apply** - submit the form with your video link, integration name, redirect URIs, and use case.
4. **Review** - our team reviews for fit and capacity. A good video usually unlocks staging during review.
5. **Get access** - receive API credentials and onboarding docs once approved.
6. **Ship** - build against live Swiggy APIs and stay in touch via `builders@swiggy.in`.

[Apply now →](https://mcp.swiggy.com/builders/access/)

## Benefits for builders and partners

Builders Club goes beyond endpoint access. The program is packaged with:

- **Live API access** with documented quotas - see [rate limits](https://mcp.swiggy.com/builders/docs/operate/rate-limits.md) - and the option to request a custom ceiling for production workloads
- **Direct engineering support** and builder enablement
- **Co-branding** opportunities for standout projects
- **Growth partnership** support for successful use cases
- A path to participate in a growing ecosystem around **agentic commerce**, skills, and future platform distribution

In short: if MCP was about making Swiggy's commerce primitives available, **Builders Club is about helping a broader ecosystem actually build, collaborate, and grow on top of them**.

> "Builders Club is not a repeat of the MCP launch. It is the next layer on top of it - a program for developers, startups, and enterprises to build, partner, and contribute to Swiggy's emerging AI commerce ecosystem."
